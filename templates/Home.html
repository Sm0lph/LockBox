From your keyboard to the database

Every entry in your vault goes through the same three steps.

  1. Sign in

    Your master password unlocks the vault. The server only keeps an Argon2 hash to check it against.

  2. Encrypt in the browser

    An AES-256 key is derived from your master password with PBKDF2, and each entry is encrypted with it before it is sent.

  3. Store only ciphertext

    The database saves the encrypted value and its IV. Reveal or copy an entry and it is decrypted in your browser again.
